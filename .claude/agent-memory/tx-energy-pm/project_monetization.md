Revenue stack in priority order:

1. **REP affiliate / lead-gen commissions (primary)** — $40–$125 per successful enrollment is the industry range, varies by REP and contract length. This is the dominant revenue line for v1.
2. **Renewal/switch alerts as recurring driver** — same affiliate revenue but repeatable annually per user. This is what makes the unit economics actually work vs one-shot acquisition.
3. **B2B data products (year 2+)** — anonymized usage and switching-behavior data sold to REPs, analysts, or regulators. Highest-margin line but requires meaningful data scale first.
4. **Premium consumer tier (optional)** — multi-property management for landlords, advanced alerts, solar/EV optimization. Probably <10% of revenue, useful for brand and retention signals.
5. **White-label / embedded comparison widget** — let realtors, moving-services, utilities-concierge apps embed our engine. Channel play, not a primary line.

**Why:** Lead-gen is the only scalable revenue from day one because every user who enrolls is a transaction. Subscription consumer revenue in this category has historically been weak — consumers don't pay monthly to "save money on electricity."

**How to apply:** Architect the enrollment flow as a first-class product surface, not an afterthought. Build attribution and revenue tracking into the data model from day one. Track LTV by acquisition channel from week one.
