Primary moat lever: **personalized true-cost ranking** powered by bill-upload OCR + usage-curve modeling. PowerToChoose and most affiliate aggregators rank by sticker price at fixed kWh tiers. Our advantage is showing each user their *actual* dollar cost on their own consumption pattern.

Secondary moats (in priority order):
1. **Data moat** — historical plan pricing time-series, plan accuracy scoring, parsed-bill corpus across TDUs
2. **Trust moat** — transparent methodology, "show your work" cost breakdowns, verified user reviews tied to actual enrollments
3. **SEO moat** — hyperlocal pages (city + ZIP + TDU + usage band), calculators, plan-change guides
4. **Switching moat** — saved profiles, renewal alerts 60/30/7 days out, annual auto re-optimization
5. **Network effects** — user reviews + plan accuracy reports (did the bill match the quoted price?)

**Why:** Affiliate aggregators are commodity. The only sustainable advantage is owning the *personalization layer* between consumer and REP — that's also what creates the highest-conversion enrollments, which is what REPs pay for.

**How to apply:** When prioritizing, prefer features that compound data assets or deepen personalization. Defer features that any competitor could ship in a week (e.g., generic plan filters).
